Established in 2009 by Dhanekula Venkata Subbaiah Charitable Trust, DIET is dedicated to providing outcome-based technical education, research excellence, and disciplined character formation in Andhra Pradesh.

Institutional Vision

Pioneering engineering education with world-class infrastructure, high-caliber faculty, and industry-collaborative learning to produce globally competent, ethical, and socially conscious engineering professionals.

  • To impart quality undergraduate and postgraduate technical education fostering innovative problem-solving.
  • To maintain rigorous academic tracking and continuous student performance improvement via structured syllabus management.
  • To cultivate ethical leadership, research aptitude, and lifelong learning capabilities.
